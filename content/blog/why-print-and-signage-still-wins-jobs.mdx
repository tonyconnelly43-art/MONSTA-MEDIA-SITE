It's easy to assume print is dead in a digital-first world, but the numbers say otherwise. According to a
[FedEx Office survey](https://newsroom.fedex.com/newsroom/global-english/fedex-office-survey-standout-signs-contribute-to-sales),
76% of consumers have entered a business they'd never visited before based on its signage, and 68% have
bought something because a sign caught their eye. For home service companies, that same instinct shows up
every time a homeowner sees a yard sign in a neighbor's lawn or a work van parked on their street.

## Yard Signs After Every Job

A yard sign left behind after a job is one of the cheapest, most effective ads a trades business can run.
Neighbors see it, ask about it, and remember the name when they need the same work done. See how yard
signs fit into a full [print and signage package](/services) alongside everything else your brand needs.

## Vehicle Signage Backs Up the Van Wrap

Print isn't limited to yard signs. Door decals, magnetic signs, and window graphics all reinforce the same
identity your [van wrap](/branding/van-wrap-design-for-contractors) is already building on the road. The
goal is the same vehicle, the same story, told from every angle.

## Business Cards and Door Hangers Still Get Passed Around

A homeowner who liked your work is far more likely to hand a neighbor a business card than to remember
and repeat a business name from memory. Door hangers work the same way for canvassing a neighborhood after
a job. Simple, cheap, and still effective.

## It Only Works If Everything Matches

None of this moves the needle if your yard signs, business cards, van, and website all look like they
belong to different companies. A [study on brand consistency](https://www.marq.com/blog/brand-consistency-competitive-advantage/)
found that businesses with consistent branding across every touchpoint saw revenue increase by up to 33%
compared to those with mismatched, inconsistent materials. Every print piece needs to pull from the same
colors, fonts, and system as the rest of your brand &mdash; not be designed in isolation.
