When someone searches "HVAC company near me," they're not landing on a website first &mdash; they're
looking at a Google Business Profile. According to
[research on local search behavior](https://www.semupdate.com/local-seo/local-searches-turn-into-customers-fast/),
76% of people who run a near-me search on their phone visit that business within 24 hours. For home
service companies, that profile is doing more selling than almost anything else you own.

## It's the First Impression, Not the Website

By the time a homeowner clicks through to a website, they've usually already formed an opinion from the
profile: the photos, the reviews, the name, and the logo sitting next to it in the map results. A profile
with a blurry logo, no photos, or a mismatched business name does the same damage a bad website does &mdash;
it just does it earlier, before you ever get the chance to make your case.

## Photos Need to Match the Brand on the Truck

The photos on a Google Business Profile should look like they belong to the same company as the
[van wrap](/branding/van-wrap-design-for-contractors) parked in the driveway and the uniform the tech is
wearing. A profile full of stock photos or outdated job site pictures breaks that consistency right at the
moment a customer is deciding whether to call.

## Reviews Carry More Weight Than Any Ad

Star ratings and review count sit right in the search results, before a single word of your copy gets
read. A five-star badge next to a recognizable, well-designed profile reads as established and trustworthy.
The same rating sitting under a generic or incomplete listing barely registers. The profile is the frame
the reviews sit inside.
