Ask five different wrap companies how much return a van wrap generates and you'll get five different
numbers &mdash; and most of them trace back to the company's own marketing material, not independent
research. That doesn't mean a wrap isn't working. It means most contractors have no real way of knowing
one way or the other, because almost nobody actually tracks it.

## Build In a Way to Track It From Day One

The fix is simpler than it sounds: give the wrap its own trackable element before it ever goes on the
vehicle. A dedicated phone number that forwards to your main line, a short vanity URL, or a QR code placed
on the wrap all let you isolate calls and visits that came specifically from someone seeing the van, not
from your website, a referral, or a Google search. Without one of these, every lead just gets credited to
"word of mouth" by default, which makes the wrap look invisible even when it's working.

## If You're Running Digital Ads Too

If you're also running paid ads or pointing people to a specific landing page from the wrap,
[Google's own URL builder](https://support.google.com/analytics/answer/10917952) lets you tag that link
with campaign parameters so it shows up as its own source in your analytics instead of blending into
general traffic. The same QR code on the van can point straight to that tagged link.

## Amortize the Cost Before Judging It

A full wrap is a multi-year asset, not a one-time expense, so judging it against a single month of leads
isn't a fair comparison. Spreading the cost over the five to seven years a quality wrap's vinyl and
lamination are realistically built to last turns a few thousand dollars upfront into a much smaller
monthly number, and makes it easier to see whether even a small, steady trickle of leads is covering it.
Our breakdown of [what a van wrap actually costs](/blog/how-much-does-a-van-wrap-cost) is a good place to
start if you haven't priced one out yet.

## What We'd Actually Look At

Rather than chasing an industry-wide ROI percentage that nobody can fully verify, we tell clients to watch
two things: whether call volume on the tracked number holds steady or grows month over month, and whether
new customers mention seeing the van unprompted. Neither one requires special software, just a dedicated
number and the habit of asking "how did you hear about us?" on every call.
